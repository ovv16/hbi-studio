    Good to Know

    Micro K-Tip, answered.

    The questions we hear most before a first consultation. Anything else, just text us.

    Book a Consultation →
    What is the Micro K-Tip method?
    K-Tip extensions are a professional strand-by-strand method designed to create natural-looking length, volume, and beautiful movement. Each individual strand features a small keratin tip that is carefully applied to your natural hair, creating discreet bonds that blend seamlessly for a comfortable, undetectable result. The method allows for customized placement and a lightweight feel while maintaining the natural beauty and integrity of your own hair when properly installed and maintained — ideal for a natural, luxurious transformation with long-lasting results.
    How long do Micro K-Tip extensions last?
    With proper care and maintenance, high-quality Micro K-Tip extensions can be reused for 9–12 months or even longer. To keep them beautiful and to protect the health of your natural hair, I recommend scheduling a reinstallation every 3–3.5 months — the extensions are carefully removed, refreshed, and reapplied for the best results. Longevity also depends on your at-home care routine, the products you use, and how gently you treat your extensions.
    Are keratin Micro K-Tips damage-free?
    Keratin Micro K-Tip extensions are designed to be a gentle, comfortable method when applied correctly by a trained professional. The small, lightweight keratin bonds distribute weight evenly and allow for natural movement while minimizing stress on your natural hair. Proper installation, regular maintenance, and following recommended aftercare are essential to keeping your natural hair healthy and achieving the best long-term results.
    Will the color match my natural hair?
    Yes. We specialize in creating customized color matches for a seamless blend with your natural hair. We can create custom color mixes and recreate even complex color techniques to match your desired look, with a wide range of shades and textures for a natural, personalized result. Whether your hair is a natural shade, highlighted, balayage, or a more complex color, we customize the extensions to blend beautifully.
    Do Micro K-Tips work for fine or thin hair?
    Yes — Micro K-Tip extensions can be an excellent option for fine or thin hair when applied correctly and customized to your natural hair. The small, discreet bonds allow for precise placement and a natural blend without a bulky appearance. We carefully select the appropriate amount of hair and placement to maintain comfort and protect your hair’s health. A professional consultation is essential to determine the right length, volume, and extension amount for your hair type.
    Why premium Slavic hair?
    Premium Slavic hair is known for its exceptional softness, natural shine, and luxurious quality. Its fine texture and smooth cuticle structure blend beautifully with many natural hair types for a seamless, natural-looking result. Our Slavic hair is carefully selected for its durability, beautiful movement, and ability to maintain its softness and appearance with proper care — an investment in a more natural look, comfortable wear, and long-lasting results.
