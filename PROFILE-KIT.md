## Полное описание

I'm Inna, a hair extension specialist with over 9 years of experience. My first salon was in Kyiv, and since 2024 I have been working with clients in Austin from my private studio off Hwy 183.

I work with Custom K-Tip extensions: small keratin bonds placed strand by strand, so the hair moves like your own and the bonds stay invisible. I use premium Slavic hair and match it to your color and texture during a free consultation.

Every visit starts with a free 20 to 30 minute consultation, and pricing is confirmed before you commit. By appointment only.
