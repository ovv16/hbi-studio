01Security

One issue here outranks everything else on the list.

Critical

Telegram bot token is exposed in the page source

main.js line 452 contains the live bot token and both chat IDs in plain text. On a static site every visitor can read that file. Anyone who does can send messages as your bot, read the message history, or delete the webhook — and bots get scraped from public repos automatically, usually within days.

FixRevoke the token in @BotFather now (/revoke), then move the send behind a free serverless proxy — Cloudflare Worker or Netlify Function — so the token lives in an env var, never in the browser. Simplest alternative: swap the form to Formspree or Basin (free tier, no backend) and forward to email.

02Trust & legal

The site currently makes claims the content doesn't back up. This is the second launch blocker.

Critical

Stock photos presented as real client work

The Before & After block is headed "Real People · Real Results" and reads "Every client. Every result. Documented honestly." — but the before and after images are two different Unsplash models. The same applies to all 7 gallery tiles (under your real Instagram handle), the bleed hero and the contact photo.

Under FTC advertising rules a before/after in a beauty context has to be a genuine result. As written this is a misrepresentation, and clients who reverse-image-search will find the stock originals.

FixReplace with real before/after pairs and real gallery photos before launch. If a slot has no real photo yet, drop the slot rather than filling it — or soften the copy to "Studio inspiration" until you have the shots.
Critical

The three testimonials appear to be invented

"Megan S.", "Ashley T." and "Lauren B." are marked with a Google glyph, implying they're pulled from your Google profile. Fabricated testimonials are the single most common FTC enforcement target for small service businesses.

FixCopy three real quotes verbatim from your Google reviews and use the reviewers' real first names. You have 5.0 stars — the real ones will read better anyway.
High

Review count says 13; brand facts say 12

The visible score block, its aria-label, and the aggregateRating in structured data all say 13 reviews. Your brand sheet records 12. Beyond the inconsistency: Google's structured-data policy forbids self-serving aggregateRating markup for your own business, and a mismatch against the live Google profile can get rich results suppressed.

FixConfirm the real count on your Google profile and use it in all three places — or remove the aggregateRating block entirely and keep only the visible link. Google shows the real rating from your Business Profile regardless.
High

Unverified stats: "500+ Happy Clients" and "9+"

Both sit in the About block as hard numbers. If they're estimates, round-and-qualify them; if they're accurate, keep them.

FixConfirm both, or reword to something defensible ("Hundreds of clients", "9 years experience").

03SEO & deployment

High

Three competing homepage files in the project

index.html, index-nano-ktip.html and index-nano-ktip-v2.html all exist at root. index.html is an older draft (it still says 12 reviews and has different content). If anything ever serves from the project root instead of build/, visitors land on the stale version — and search engines can index both as duplicates.

FixKeep index-nano-ktip-v2.html as the single source of truth, archive the other two out of root, and deploy only from build/.
High

build/ is out of date

The build folder holds its own copies of style.css, main.js and index.html. Recent edits to the source (including today's footer alignment fix) aren't in them, so the deployed site and the working file have drifted.

FixRe-copy source → build before every deploy. Worth writing the step down in build/README.md so it isn't skipped.
Medium

Sitemap is incomplete and stale

sitemap.xml lists only the homepage — privacy.html and terms.html are missing — and lastmod reads 2026-07-07.

FixAdd both legal pages and refresh lastmod on each deploy.

FixExport a 1200×630 share card: hero photo, gold wordmark, "Micro K-Tip Extensions · Austin, TX".

Canonical URL still on the GitHub Pages address

FixUpdate all four the day the custom domain goes live.

04Performance

High

No image dimensions anywhere → layout shift

Not one <img> carries width/height. Every image reflows the page as it arrives, which is exactly what Google's CLS metric penalises — and on a photo-heavy site the cumulative shift will be large.

FixAdd intrinsic width/height attributes, or set aspect-ratio on each image class in CSS. Cheapest big win on the list.
Medium

Hero image isn't prioritised

The hero fallback is your Largest Contentful Paint element but has no fetchpriority="high" and isn't preloaded — the browser discovers it late, after CSS.

FixAdd fetchpriority="high" to .cine-img and a <link rel="preload" as="image"> for assets/hero-fallback-800.webp in the head.
Medium

Twelve font weights loaded, far fewer used

The Google Fonts request pulls Cormorant Garamond in 6 cuts (incl. italics) and Inter in 5. Each is a separate download blocking first paint.

FixTrim the request to the weights actually in the CSS — likely Cormorant 400/500 + 400i/500i and Inter 300/400/500/600.
Medium

Photos load from Unsplash's CDN at runtime

Gallery, before/after, bleed hero and contact all hotlink Unsplash. That's a third-party dependency on your critical path, and the images vanish if a photo is taken down. It resolves itself when you swap in real photos — worth doing then, not twice.

FixHost the final photos yourself in assets/ as sized WebP.

05Accessibility

High

The custom cursor hides the system cursor site-wide

cursor: none !important is applied to every link, button, summary and label. Anyone who relies on the OS cursor — low vision, tremor, a magnifier, or just a bright room — loses their pointer entirely. It's also the most likely thing to make an older client bounce.

FixGate it behind @media (pointer: fine) and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference), and add a visible off switch in the tweaks panel that persists.
High

The custom service dropdown has no keyboard support

It's a <button> plus a role="listbox" list, but main.js registers no arrow-key, Enter, Home/End or Escape handling on it (only the lightbox has key handling). Keyboard and screen-reader users cannot choose a service — which means they cannot complete the booking form.

FixAdd ArrowUp/Down to move the active option, Enter/Space to select, Escape to close and return focus to the trigger, plus aria-activedescendant. Or fall back to a native <select> styled to match — far less code.
Medium

Gallery tiles are links to nowhere

All seven are <a href="#">. A screen reader announces a link; activating it jumps to the top of the page if the lightbox script hasn't loaded.

FixMake them <button type="button"> since they open a lightbox — or point href at the real Instagram post so they work without JS.
Medium

Muted text may fail contrast

--mute-2 (#807666) on --ink (#0D0B09) is roughly 4.2:1 — under the 4.5:1 minimum for body text. --mute (#B0A595) passes comfortably.

FixReserve --mute-2 for large text and hairlines only; use --mute wherever it carries small copy.
Medium

Loose ends

  • TikTok social icon is commented out awaiting a URL — either add the profile or delete the block.
  • Service modal still shows the "Add service photo" placeholder for every service.
  • Before/After block still shows the "Drop your before/after pair via the slots panel" authoring hint — make sure it can never surface to a visitor.
  • The second Telegram recipient (164306473) still needs to press Start on the bot before it can receive anything.

06What's already right

Worth stating, because a lot of this is done well and shouldn't be touched:

  • Structured data is thorough — HairSalon with address, hours, phone, sameAs, plus a complete FAQPage.
  • Exactly one <h1>; the alternate hero variants correctly use <p class="hero-h1">.
  • Skip link, aria-labeled landmarks, real form labels, correct alt="" on the decorative hair layer.
  • Responsive srcset/sizes on every photo, and loading="lazy" below the fold.
  • Hero video degrades to an animated still on save-data and slow connections.
  • Lightbox has full keyboard support: Escape, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight.
  • Brand facts — phone, email, address, Instagram handle, Micro K-Tip naming — are consistent throughout. No stray "hand-tied".
